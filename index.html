The scale of the problem

430M metric tons of plastic produced annually — set to triple by 2060
PET bottle waste takes up 25% of space in any dumpsite
PET bottles take 500–1000 years to decompose
Africa & Asia account for 90% of ocean plastic waste
Africa produces ~6% of global plastic, but imports 70% of what it consumes
Africa is grappling with circularity and plastic waste mismanagement

Our integrated recycling system

Our integrated PET bottle waste recycling system maximises the value of PET waste, minimises waste generation, conserves resources, and reduces environmental impact — creating a circular economy where PET bottles are recycled, reused and transformed into valuable products.

1. Feeding System — loading hopper, inclined conveyor
2. Separation Line — magnetic tumbler, sorting conveyor
3. Crushing Line — dry & wet crusher, buffer silo, gravity separator
4. Washing & Drying Line — hot/cold wash, dewatering, hydro cyclone, hot air dryer
5. Products Silo
6. End products — PET flakes, polyester staple fibre, film & strap

What recycled PET becomes

Polyester staple fibre (solid, regular hollow, hollow conjugated), PET film and PET strap feed into home furnishing and toy stuffing, mattresses, pillows and bedding, non-woven textiles, and padding and wadding materials — which in turn become geotextiles, acoustic and thermal insulation, and carpet underlay.
