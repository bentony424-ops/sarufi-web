Privacy Policy

Last updated: 13th September 2026

Sarufi Energy Group ("Sarufi", "we", "us", "our") respects your privacy. This policy explains what personal data we collect through this website, why we collect it, and how you can control it. It applies to this website only.

1. Who we are

Sarufi Energy Group is based in Nairobi, Kenya, at Nyati Sacco Plaza, 8th Floor, Madaraka Roundabout, Nairobi West, P.O. Box 27336 – 00100, Nairobi, Kenya. You can reach us at info@sarufigroup.com or +254 717 501501 for any question about this policy or your data.

2. What we collect

This site does not require an account, and does not process payments. The only personal data we collect directly is what you choose to give us through the contact form:

  • Your name
  • Your email address
  • Your phone number, if you provide one (optional)
  • The service you're interested in, and the message you write

We also use a privacy-focused website analytics tool that reports aggregate visit statistics (e.g. page views, general location by country/region, device type). It does not use cookies and does not identify individual visitors.

This site does not use cookies. We don't run advertising trackers, and our analytics provider does not set cookies or build profiles of individual visitors. If that changes in future (for example, if we add a marketing analytics tool that does use cookies), we'll update this policy and add a consent banner before doing so.

3. How we use your data

We use the information you submit through the contact form solely to respond to your enquiry — for example, to discuss a potential solar installation or a PET waste recycling partnership. We do not sell your data, and we do not use it for marketing unless you separately and clearly agree to that.

4. Who we share it with

We use the following third-party service providers to run this website. Each only receives the minimum data needed to perform its function:

  • Web3Forms — delivers contact form submissions to our email inbox. Web3Forms processes the content of your submission on our behalf; it does not use it for its own purposes.
  • Cloudflare — provides our website analytics (cookieless, aggregate visit statistics) and, where used, network security services.
  • GitHub — hosts the website files themselves.

Because these providers operate infrastructure outside Kenya, your data may be processed on servers located in other countries. We only use providers who maintain appropriate technical and organisational safeguards for personal data.

5. How long we keep it

We keep contact form submissions only for as long as needed to respond to your enquiry and for reasonable business record-keeping afterwards, after which we delete it. If you'd like your data deleted sooner, contact us and we'll action that promptly.

6. Your rights

Under Kenya's Data Protection Act, 2019, you have the right to:

  • Ask us what personal data we hold about you, and get a copy of it
  • Ask us to correct inaccurate or incomplete data
  • Ask us to delete your data, where there's no legitimate reason for us to keep processing it
  • Object to how we're processing your data, or ask us to restrict it
  • Request that we transfer your data to you or another provider, where technically feasible

To exercise any of these rights, email info@sarufigroup.com. If you're not satisfied with how we've handled your data, you also have the right to lodge a complaint with the Office of the Data Protection Commissioner (ODPC).

7. Security

We take reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect the data you share with us against loss, misuse, or unauthorised access. No method of transmission over the internet is completely secure, but we work to protect your information to industry standards.

8. Children's privacy

This website is intended for institutional clients, businesses, and members of the public seeking information about our services, and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect personal data from anyone under 18.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time — for example, if we add a new tool to the website that changes what data we collect. We'll update the "last updated" date above whenever we do.

10. Contact us

Questions about this policy or your data can be sent to info@sarufigroup.com or +254 717 501501.
